Questions

What if I don't like it?

One revision is included. If the second version still misses, we refund the $49.

Can it be a surprise?

Yes. Everything goes to your email only. Play it for them whenever you want.

How long should my story be?

Three or four true things is enough. The best songs come from one specific memory, not a summary of a whole life.
